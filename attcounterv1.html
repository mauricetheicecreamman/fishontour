How to use it

Enter the date, time, home or away status, opponent, competition and volunteer name before opening the gate. Tap the large button once for each attendee. Use Undo for an accidental tap. Save the match at the end and download the CSV.

Storage: records remain only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data removes them.

Standalone prototype. No account or connection is required after the page loads.
